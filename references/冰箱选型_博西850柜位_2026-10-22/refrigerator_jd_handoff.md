## 6. Cabinet fit: the critical unresolved issue

### The user's actual design

**850 W × 600 D × 1935 H mm** is the dimension already sent to the designer. Do not silently replace it with the earlier proposed 1950 mm height.

The remaining check is whether these are the **actual finished clear dimensions**, and whether the 600 mm depth is measured to the intended adjacent cabinet-door front plane. A nominal cabinet depth is not sufficient evidence of an unobstructed refrigerator installation envelope.

### Arithmetic only — not installation approval

| Previously reported body size | Remaining total width / centred side gap | Height difference to 1935 | Depth difference to 600 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 842 × 594 × 1925 | 8 mm total / 4 mm per side | 10 mm | 6 mm |
| 841 × 599 × 1915 | 9 mm total / 4.5 mm per side | 20 mm | 1 mm |
| 835 × 599 × 1915 | 15 mm total / 7.5 mm per side | 20 mm | 1 mm |

These numbers only subtract the reported body dimensions from the opening. They do **not** establish sufficient ventilation, rear clearance, door sweep, levelling allowance or installation tolerance.

### Rules for the final compatibility decision

- Obtain the **official installation drawing for the exact model/E-Nr variant**. Record minimum opening width, depth and height separately from appliance body dimensions.
- Check levelling feet, floor level, wall squareness, manufacturing/installation tolerances and any top hinge or cover protrusions.
- Confirm what the published depth includes, and whether rear spacers, required rear clearance, plugs or cables increase installed depth.
- Check the door opening envelope beside the actual adjoining cabinet or wall, and whether drawers can be used and removed at the available opening angle.
- Preserve every ventilation path required by the manufacturer. Do not add side/top trim or a plinth cover solely to hide gaps without checking the installation instructions.
- Position the outlet and cable so they remain accessible and do not consume required space behind the appliance; have the designer/installer confirm the permitted arrangement.
- If the official **minimum** opening height exceeds 1935 mm, the model does **not** qualify for the current fixed opening. Mark it **requires cabinet change**, not “fits because its body is only 1925 mm.”
- A removable trim piece helps only if there is genuinely greater unobstructed structural height behind it. Removing decorative trim cannot create space through a fixed shelf.

### Corrections to earlier advice

The prior conversation was too confident that “850 × 600 × 1935 fits them all,” and that 594/599 mm appliance depth guarantees flush installation in a 600 mm opening. **Neither conclusion is established.**

Likewise, a bottom-venting description does not by itself prove that a 10 mm top gap is acceptable, and a 4 mm advertised side gap does not eliminate installation tolerances.

Keep the user's 850 mm width as the current brief, but do not call it universally sufficient or describe a wider opening as inherently wrong without an exact-model installation check.
